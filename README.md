## About

I study structural risks at the intersection of **human cognition, machine speed, behavioural risk, and digital decision systems** — particularly *edge-decision environments*, where time pressure, information density, emotional vulnerability, and machine-speed systems affect human decision-making.

My approach is **architectural, not algorithmic**: I examine how system design can create, amplify, or reduce risk at the point where human decisions meet digital systems, rather than proposing new algorithms.

- 🎓 B.Sc. Statistics, University of Madras (1992–1996)
- 🧭 25 years in payroll systems & operational environments — the practical grounding for this research
- 🔬 Method: literature review, systems analysis, prototype development, structured self-observation, and critical discussion with reviewers and collaborators. AI language models are used as critique/drafting instruments — **not** as a substitute for empirical validation or formal peer review.

## 🧭 Peer-Review Roadmap

This repository is being built to support a transition from open preprint → independent review → formal publication. Current milestones:

- [ ] Select 1–2 flagship papers (e.g., Swayambu Triad, HTCP) for formal statistical/methodological review
- [ ] Engage independent reviewers/collaborators (see [Collaboration](#-collaboration))
- [ ] Run controlled empirical tests using the open instruments (HMTS Testing Suite, IDD Suite, TCMT Stress Lab)
- [ ] Submit revised manuscript(s) to an appropriate peer-reviewed journal or conference
- [ ] Document outcomes transparently, including results that weaken or contradict the frameworks
